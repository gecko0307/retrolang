# XA Music

Use [psxavenc](https://github.com/WonderfulToolchain/psxavenc) and [XA Tool](/xatool) to convert your own music to PlayStation XA format.

Provided `convert.bat` assumes two files, `channel0.mp3` and `channel1.mp3`, that are converted and interleaved into one `FILE0.XA` which is written to the disc.
